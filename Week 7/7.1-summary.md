**One line:** Course wrap-up. Ethics is **everyone's job**, not
just specialists'. Small, steady actions add up to a culture of
ethical AI.

**Where it fits:** the finish line. It stitches together every
module: fairness, accountability, dignity, transparency,
privacy, explainability, governance.

# LAYER 1: LEARN IT

## The big idea
Knowing the principles isn't enough. The goal is to **use**
them: spot problems, speak up, and show that **ethics and
innovation go together.**

**Hook:** **See it, say it, show it.** Spot misuse, speak up,
be the role model.

## What the lecture says to carry forward
1. **The tour:** technology + **morality** + **societal impact**
   interact. You studied **fairness, accountability, human
   dignity, transparency** (and more) and their role in **trust
   and safety.**
2. **Real-world scenarios** showed AI's influence on **privacy,
   bias, and decision making.**
3. **The aim:** critically assess AI's ethical implications and
   feel responsible to **advocate for responsible AI development
   and use.**
4. **The vision:** shape an **AI-powered future** that honors
   your values, respects your diversity, amplifies **everyone's
   shared human experience.**
5. **Why ethics:** ensures AI **serves humanity equitably,
   preventing harm and fostering trust.**
6. **How change happens:** **small, consistent actions by
   individuals** create momentum toward a culture of ethical AI.
7. **Who owns it:** AI ethics is **not just for specialists**;
   it's **everyone's responsibility.**
8. **What to do:**
   - **Speak up** about **data misuse, discriminatory models,
     harmful applications.**
   - **Be a role model:** ethics and innovation go hand in hand.

## Big picture recap
- Ethics = **practice**, not just theory.
- Everyone is responsible; speak up.
- Small actions -> culture change.
