Frameworks. What is a framework? A framework provides the frame to contextualize information. A framework is a structured, overarching system or model that outlines the key elements, core values, and related principles that steer the goals. It provides a conceptual structure or architecture that shapes the audience's approach and thought on a topic. It provides the high-level roadmap or defines the components and their relationships. It is an architecture to guide decision-making and ensure alignment with broader goals. A framework serves as a foundation for standards, policies, and guidance. Frameworks are those that are normally put into practice in the absence of well-defined standards and laws.
