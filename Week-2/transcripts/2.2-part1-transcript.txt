Keeping in mind that a technical standard conveys an official rule or an accepted protocol or a way of operating, standards provide specifications of guidelines or requirements, taxonomies are a closed list of acceptable terms that are arranged hierarchically and used to describe and classify content. While not every standard or framework has a taxonomy, glossaries and definitions and terms provide classification.

AI Impact Assessments (AI-IAs) are a way of identifying positive and negative impacts early on to safeguard AI's benefits and avoid its downsides. There are different views of what constitutes or is conceptualized as an AIIA. Their tools can take the form of self-assessments that can be used for various purposes, such as audits and meeting legal or other requirements and standards. The AIIA covers a broad range of issues that can be grouped into categories such as human rights, ethics, data protection, privacy, security, safety, and environmental impacts. AIIAs can be embedded in organizational processes and structures in different ways, can be part of broader governance system that contributes to AI's responsible governance.

AI impact assessments (AI-IAs) and ethics impact assessments (EIAs) as well as standards are not neutral. They should be treated with discernment over the implicit and explicit taxonomies, which operationalize certain values, and in doing so, often miss the true harms. For example, within an impact assessment, a taxonomy might provide a specific system to identify and categorize who the specific people, groups, or entities at risk in a way to categorize different types of harms, and in doing so, provide a system to prioritize certain and specific stakeholders risks and harms.

In this scenario, AI developers are working for government-owned corporation in Finland. They are tasked with creating a transportation system that realizes the goal of no personal ownership of vehicles by 2040. Because Finland was deemed the happiest country in the world by the World Happiness Report in 2018 through 2022, the city has asked AI developers to be sure to include satisfaction with life as an indicator to be used by the WHR.

AI developers are planning a pilot study on an island community that approved the plan via a vote. Almost 80% of the population of the island already relies completely on public transportation within and outside the island. Thus, only about 5,000 people are changing their habit for the study. For the duration of one month, the 5,000 car owners will not use their personal cars for any transportation on the island.

What is the AIS? The AIS service is the transportation system designed to provide the people of Finland with an integrated transportation network that uses a variety of AI AVs, and non-AI, such as bike share systems, in order to eliminate the need for private car ownership. This system should be able to compete with private vehicle ownership in terms of both cost and convenience.

Some of the factors AIS creators considered during the initial task analysis. Vehicles will emit zero greenhouse gas emissions. The vehicles will be designed in a way that they are accessible to people with disabilities. The vehicles will use sensor systems that allow for increased safety. The technology incorporated inside the vehicle will protect user privacy. The interaction between people and the vehicles need to incorporate explainable and interpretable communication interfaces. Some of the discussed product features include social interactions between the AV and people using vehicles with faces, whereby the light represents eyes and the bumper represents the mouth. The lights will both pick up expressions from pedestrian faces and display the intent of the vehicles.

Riders in the vehicles will have the option to choose the nature of the ride. Riders can also choose to log personal preferences for music, talk shows, or other entertainment when sharing rides, music and so forth. They both like to be played if they choose. Riders can choose to spend their AV commute time learning new skills, relaxing or engaging in other activities. The AV encourages them to learn new skills.

What is the need it meets and what is the problem it solves? The AI system is designed to fulfill the goal of the city and to give people transportation in an urban environment than they currently have with their personal vehicles. The automated vehicles interactions between the AV system and people, so the AV sensors pick up expressions and body movements from pedestrians, faces, movements, and behaviors, so this data can provide feedback on non-riders, pedestrians, and bike traffic.

Since happiness and human well-being are at the forefront of this project, the AI development team will also use a variety of population, health, employment, and educational data, as well as data they can obtain from social media, gaming and streaming data. In the future, as new components and users become integrated into the system, the team expects to link a variety of smart home sensors and health networks to assess the family and individual relationships which are essential for human well-being.
