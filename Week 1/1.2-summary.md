# 1.2 Floridi: Ethics Washing
*Read time: 2 min*

**One line:** Stop only debating *what* ethical standards
are. Start *doing* them. Floridi names 5 ways companies
fake it.

**Hook:** **Buffet, Paint, Lawyer, Trash, Skip**

## The 5 risks

| Hook   | Risk            | Plain meaning                   |
|--------|-----------------|---------------------------------|
| Buffet | Ethics shopping | Pick the rule that fits what you already do |
| Paint  | Bluewashing     | Look ethical without real change |
| Lawyer | Ethics lobbying | Use "ethics" to delay or weaken laws |
| Trash  | Ethics dumping  | Do abroad what's banned at home |
| Skip   | Ethics shirking | Skip ethics where payoff is low |

## One example each
- **Shopping:** Facebook gave EU users strong GDPR
  privacy, but moved ~1.5B non-EU users to looser
  US-style terms.
- **Bluewashing:** Facebook in Myanmar. Its impact
  assessment skipped the Rohingya, and fixes looked
  like PR, not root causes.
  (Greenwashing = same trick for the environment.)
- **Lobbying:** Google and allies pushed to soften
  California's CCPA, saying "we self-regulate."
- **Dumping:** Huawei/ZTE smart cities in Africa with
  facial recognition and weak privacy laws. Locals
  become a **testing ground**.
- **Shirking:** PredPol knew its data was biased and
  looped, but did little to fix it. Vulnerable
  communities pay.

## The takeaway
Rules and company policy aren't enough. Your **personal
values** are the compass when no one is checking.

## 30-second recall
Can you name the 5 and give a one-line meaning for each?
