The principle of responsibility refers to the ethical obligation of individuals, organizations, and governments to act with accountability In the development, deployment and use of AI systems, it emphasizes recognizing the potential societal, environmental, and economic impacts of AI and taking proactive steps to prevent harm. The principle of governance refers to the systems, policies and frameworks that regulate the development, deployment, and use of AI technologies. Governance ensures that AI systems are designed and operated in ways that align with societal values, protect individuals, and promote fairness, accountability, and transparency.

The ethical AI principle of sustainability refers to the development, deployment and use of AI technologies in a way that preserve environmental resources to minimize ecological harm and to ensure that future generations can enjoy the benefits of ai. The principles of responsibility, governance and sustainability are deeply interconnected, forming a cohesive framework for ethical and impactful AI development.
